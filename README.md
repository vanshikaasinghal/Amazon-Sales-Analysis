# 📊 Amazon Sales Analysis
End-to-End Data Analysis using SQL & Power BI

## 🚀 Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing Amazon sales data to uncover meaningful business insights related to revenue, product performance, category trends, and customer reviews.

The objective was to transform raw sales data into interactive and actionable insights using SQL and Power BI.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement
E-commerce platforms generate massive amounts of data.

The challenge was to:

🔹 Identify top-performing categories and products

🔹 Analyze revenue and profit margins

🔹 Compare Organic vs Sponsored sales

🔹 Evaluate customer ratings and reviews

🔹 Present insights through a clear and interactive dashboard

---


## 📦 Dataset Overview

The project utilizes multiple structured datasets containing Amazon product and sales information:

**Products Table** – Includes product names, categories, pricing, and ratings

**Sales Data** – Contains revenue, quantity sold, and sales type (Organic/Sponsored)

**Reviews Data** – Customer ratings and total review counts

**Category Data** – Product classification and performance segmentation

**Other derived metrics include**: profit margin calculations, revenue contribution %, and category-wise comparisons.

---


## 🛠 Tools & Technologies Used

💾 **SQL** – Data extraction and analysis

📊 **Power BI** – Dashboard creation

📈 **DAX** – KPI calculations and measures

🔗 **Data Modeling** – Relationship building

🧹 **Data Cleaning** – Handling missing and inconsistent values

---


## 📌 Key KPIs Analyzed

💰 Total Revenue

📉 Profit Margin (6.23%)

🏷 Category-wise Sales

🔄 Organic vs Sponsored Sales

⭐ Total Reviews

🌟 Product Ratings

---


## 📊 Key Business Insights

🔋 Power & Batteries category generated the highest sales volume

📈 Organic sales outperformed Sponsored sales in most categories

💬 Higher review count showed strong correlation with higher sales

⭐ Product ratings were consistently above 4.2 across categories

🏆 Few top products contributed significantly to total revenue

---


## 🖥 Dashboard Features

🎛 Interactive category filters

📊 Revenue and sales comparison visuals

📅 Monthly performance tracking

🏅 Top-performing product identification

📌 KPI cards for quick business overview

---


## 🎯 Conclusion

This project successfully transformed raw Amazon sales data into actionable business insights.

It improved decision-making visibility by highlighting product performance, sales trends, and profitability while strengthening my expertise in SQL, Power BI, and analytical thinking.
